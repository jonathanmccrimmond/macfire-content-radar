## Angle

The weekly user test is the responsible person's own duty, but it is the regular professional service of the whole system that keeps it reliable, and that is what MacFire provides.

## LinkedIn

Most workplaces should test their fire alarm once a week. It takes five minutes and it is the simplest way to know the system responds before you ever need it.

A simple weekly routine:

- Use a different call point each week, so over time you cover them all.
- Let people know it is a test first, then trigger it.
- Check the alarm is clearly audible everywhere, including noisy areas and quiet corners.
- Write down the date, the call point used, and that it worked, in your fire log book.

The weekly test is your own job, and it is worth doing well. What it cannot do is keep the whole system maintained: the panel, detectors, batteries and wiring need a regular service by a qualified engineer, usually twice a year. That part is ours. If a weekly test flags a fault, or your service is due, we can sort it.
