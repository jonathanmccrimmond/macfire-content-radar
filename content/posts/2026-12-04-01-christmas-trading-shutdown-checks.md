## Angle

A practical reminder for businesses, hospitality, and facilities teams to run a short fire-safety check before peak Christmas trading and again before shutdown.

## LinkedIn

Two short fire-safety moments worth carving out before Christmas.

Before the trading peak:

- Decorations and lights kept clear of heat sources and not blocking signage or sensors.
- Stock build-up not stored in escape routes or against fire doors.
- Kitchen extraction cleaned and suppression in date.
- Staff and seasonal hires briefed on what to do if the alarm goes.

Before shutdown:

- Final lock-up walk includes heaters off, chargers unplugged, fridges and essential kit confirmed.
- Alarm monitoring contacts up to date with whoever is on call.
- Out-of-hours key holder list checked.
- Any decorations left up are well away from heaters and lighting.

Quiet five-minute jobs that often save a much louder January.
