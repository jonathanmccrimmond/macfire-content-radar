## Angle

A practical winter reminder about portable heaters, extension leads, and overloaded sockets in workshops, offices, hospitality back-of-house, and homes.

## LinkedIn

January is when portable heaters start appearing under desks, in back-of-house corridors, and in cold corners of workshops. Most of the time that is fine. The risk is the bits around the heater rather than the heater itself.

Worth a quick look:

- Heaters on the floor, not on cardboard, paper, or a pallet.
- Heaters with enough clear space around them, away from curtains, stock, and bins.
- Extension leads not daisy-chained, and not running under rugs or doormats.
- Sockets and adaptors not warm to the touch or showing scorch marks.
- Chargers and heaters switched off at the end of the day.

If a heater is doing the job that a fixed heating system should be doing, that is usually a maintenance conversation rather than a fire-safety one. Worth flagging either way.
