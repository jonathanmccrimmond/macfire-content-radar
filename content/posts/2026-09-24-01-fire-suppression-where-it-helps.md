## Angle

Some higher-risk areas need an automatic suppression system, not just extinguishers, and MacFire designs, installs and services them.

## LinkedIn

Extinguishers are the right answer for most small fires, in the right hands. But some risks move too fast, or sit somewhere nobody is standing, and there a suppression system earns its place.

Where suppression is worth considering:

- Commercial kitchens, over the cooking line, where hot oil can take hold quickly.
- Server rooms and comms cabinets, where water would do as much damage as the fire.
- Plant, machinery, and areas that run unattended.

A suppression system detects and acts in seconds, often before a person could, and is matched to the specific risk: wet chemical for kitchens, a clean agent for electronics, and so on. Designing the right system, installing it, and keeping it serviced is what we do. If you have an area where an extinguisher feels like not quite enough, that is worth a conversation.
