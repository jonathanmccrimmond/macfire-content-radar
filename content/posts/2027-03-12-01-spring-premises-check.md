## Angle

A simple seasonal prompt for facilities and business teams to clear exits, review signage, check emergency lighting, and tidy storage.

## LinkedIn

Spring is a good moment for a quick fire-safety tidy-up. None of this is dramatic, it just tends to drift over a busy winter.

A few things worth a walk-round:

- Escape routes and final exits clear and easy to open.
- Fire exit and directional signage still visible and correct.
- Emergency lighting working (most units have a test button).
- Extinguishers in place, in date, and not blocked.
- Storage tidied back, with nothing building up in corridors or by doors.

If a walk-round throws up more questions than answers, a competent review will sort the priorities.
