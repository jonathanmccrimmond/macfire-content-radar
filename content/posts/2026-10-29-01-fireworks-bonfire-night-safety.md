## Angle

A practical fire-safety reminder around Bonfire Night, covering both households running small displays at home and venues running organised events.

## LinkedIn

Bonfire Night brings two very different audiences worth a quick word for.

For households:

- Keep fireworks in a closed metal container until use.
- One person lighting, everyone else well back.
- Have a bucket of water nearby for spent fireworks.
- Keep pets indoors and check on neighbours who might find it stressful.

For venues running an organised event:

- Confirm your stewarding plan, exits, and crowd routes.
- Brief staff on what to do if the alarm goes inside while an event is on outside.
- Check extinguishers on the route between any pyrotechnic area and the building.
- Keep clear access for fire service vehicles, even when the car park is busy.

Organised displays remain the safer option for most people. Either way, a short walk round the day before catches almost everything.
