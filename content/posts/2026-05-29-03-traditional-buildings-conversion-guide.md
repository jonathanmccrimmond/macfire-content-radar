## Angle

A practical heads-up for anyone converting older Scottish buildings before or after the 6 April 2026 handbook changes.

## LinkedIn

If you work on conversions of traditional Scottish buildings, the new interim guidance from the Scottish Government is worth a careful read.

Published on 20 March 2026, the Guide to Conversion of Traditional Buildings supports the Domestic and Non-Domestic Technical Handbooks that take effect on 6 April 2026. It covers the fire-safety side in detail - separation between occupancies, structural fire protection, cavity fire stopping, internal linings, external wall fire spread, escape provisions, and fire service access and water supply.

For owners and project teams, the practical takeaway is the same as always: get the fire strategy clarified at design stage, not at sign-off. The earlier the conversation, the cheaper and calmer the project.
