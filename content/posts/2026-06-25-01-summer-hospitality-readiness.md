## Angle

A practical reminder for venues, pubs, restaurants, and event spaces to run a short fire-safety check before the busy summer trading period.

## LinkedIn

Summer trading is one of the busier stretches of the year for hospitality. It is also when small fire-safety gaps tend to get noticed.

A short pre-season walk round is usually enough:

- Kitchen suppression in date and serviced.
- Extinguishers in place, sealed, and signed.
- Escape routes clear, even when the outside seating is busy.
- Final exits unlocked during trading hours.
- Staff briefed on what to do if the alarm goes, including any new starters.

None of this needs to be dramatic. A quiet half hour with the duty manager usually surfaces anything that needs sorting before a hot Saturday night.
