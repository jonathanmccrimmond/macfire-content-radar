## LinkedIn

As the nights draw in, candles, fairy lights, and seasonal displays start appearing at home and at work. A few calm reminders.

- Keep candles away from anything that can catch, and never leave them burning unattended.
- Battery tealights are a genuinely good swap in busy or public spaces.
- Do not run decorations or extra lighting off daisy-chained extension leads.
- Keep displays, stock, and decorations clear of exits, signage, and fire equipment.

None of this needs to be dramatic. It is mostly about where things are put, and remembering to switch them off.
