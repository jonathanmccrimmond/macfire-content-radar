## Angle

A practical reminder for private landlords, letting agents, and HMO operators to run a short fire-safety check ahead of the autumn intake.

## LinkedIn

Autumn intake is a sensible moment for landlords, letting agents, and HMO operators to do a short fire-safety pass through each property.

A useful starter list:

- Smoke and heat alarms tested, batteries fresh, interlinked where required.
- Fire doors closing properly, with intact seals and no missing closers.
- Emergency lighting working on common stair and corridor routes.
- Escape routes clear of bikes, bin bags, and stored furniture.
- Tenant pack with a short "what to do if the alarm goes" page.

Most issues turn up in five minutes. Fixing them before tenants move in is much easier than fixing them in October.
