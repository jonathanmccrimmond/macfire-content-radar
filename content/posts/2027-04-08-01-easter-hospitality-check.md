## Angle

A pre-busy-period reminder for restaurants, cafes, pubs, and venues to check kitchen suppression, extinguishers, and escape routes before footfall climbs.

## LinkedIn

Easter and the spring bank holidays bring a jump in footfall for a lot of hospitality venues. A short check before it gets busy is time well spent.

Worth confirming ahead of the rush:

- Kitchen suppression and extinguishers serviced and in date.
- Escape routes clear, even with extra stock, seating, or deliveries in.
- Fire exits easy to open from the inside, and never locked during trading.
- New or seasonal staff know the alarm, the exits, and the assembly point.

Busy periods are exactly when the basics matter most.
