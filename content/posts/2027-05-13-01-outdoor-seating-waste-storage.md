## Angle

As the weather warms up, outdoor areas, bins, smoking points, and storage near buildings can create avoidable fire risks. A practical reminder for hospitality and business.

## LinkedIn

As outdoor seating and beer gardens open back up, it is worth a thought about what sits against the building.

Easy things to get right:

- Keep bins and waste storage away from walls, windows, and doors. Wheelie bins against a wall are a common cause of avoidable fire spread.
- Give designated smoking points proper, non-combustible bins, and keep them clear of seating and storage.
- Do not let cardboard, pallets, or stock pile up in outdoor corners.
- Keep escape routes from outdoor areas clear too.

Most of this is simply about where things get put down. A quick look around the back of the building usually tells the story.
