## LinkedIn

Scotland's building standards are moving again, and fire safety is right in the middle of it.

The Scottish Government says new technical handbooks come into use from 6 April 2026, with updates to Section 2 Fire, Section 3 Environment and Section 4 Safety. The same update also points to Simon and Richard's Law, which introduces new fire suppression requirements for traditionally constructed buildings converted to hotel use.

If you're planning a conversion, alteration, or change of use, this is the moment to check the fire side early. The cheapest time to fix a problem is before the work starts.
