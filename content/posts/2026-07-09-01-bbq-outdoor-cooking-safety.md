## Angle

A practical, non-dramatic public-safety reminder about outdoor cooking, disposable BBQs, and where they should and should not be used.

## LinkedIn

A few quiet reminders for the BBQ season:

- Disposable BBQs stay hot long after the food is off. Cool them fully with water before they go in a bin.
- Keep any BBQ well clear of fences, sheds, parasols, and dry grass.
- If you are out in the countryside or near woodland, leave the disposable BBQ at home.
- Pub gardens and outdoor venues: brief staff on what to do if a customer brings their own.

Most outdoor cooking is fine. The avoidable incidents tend to come from heat, wind, and where the BBQ gets put down afterwards.
