## Angle

A supportive, practical reminder for care homes and supported living to review fire doors, window restrictors, portable cooling, and evacuation plans before any warm spells.

## LinkedIn

Warmer weather changes how a care setting runs: windows open, fans plugged in, doors propped for airflow. A few things are worth a second look before any heatwave.

For care homes and supported living:

- Fire doors stay closed. If airflow is the issue, look at proper hold-open devices linked to the alarm, never wedges.
- Check window restrictors are working. They matter for both falls and safe escape.
- Make sure fans and portable cooling are not overloading sockets or trailing across escape routes.
- Revisit PEEPs (personal emergency evacuation plans) for any resident whose needs have changed.

Comfort and fire safety do not have to pull against each other. They just need a plan.
