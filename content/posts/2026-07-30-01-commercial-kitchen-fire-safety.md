## Angle

Routine cleaning reduces the risk in a commercial kitchen, but it is the right extinguishing equipment and suppression, supplied and serviced by MacFire, that actually stops a fire.

## LinkedIn

Most commercial kitchen fires come down to two things: grease and hot oil. A bit of routine keeps the risk down.

Worth staying on top of:

- Extraction and ducting collect grease, and need cleaning on a schedule that suits how hard the kitchen works.
- Hot oil is never left unattended, and is given time to cool before cleaning.
- Filters cleaned regularly, and surfaces wiped down at the end of service.

Routine reduces the risk, but it does not put a fire out. For that you need the right equipment within reach: a fire blanket and a wet chemical extinguisher made for cooking oils, never water, and, in a busy kitchen, a suppression system over the cooking line. Making sure you have the correct kit, in the right places, serviced and ready, is what we do. Happy to take a look.
