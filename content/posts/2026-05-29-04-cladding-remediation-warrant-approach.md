## Angle

What building owners and remediation teams should know about how cladding warrant applications are being checked.

## LinkedIn

If your building is in scope for cladding remediation, the verification side is starting to firm up.

On 25 March 2026, the Scottish Government published an outline approach to verifying building warrant applications for cladding remediation works. It sits on top of the 1 June 2022 amendment to the Building (Scotland) Regulations and applies to buildings 11 metres and above, with material classifications, insulation depths and masonry thicknesses all called out.

For owners and project teams, the practical move is to read the factsheet before applying. A warrant application that already lines up with the verification approach is going to move much faster than one that doesn't.
