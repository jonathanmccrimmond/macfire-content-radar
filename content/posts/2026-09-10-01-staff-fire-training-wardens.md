## Angle

Equipment only works if people know what to do; MacFire delivers practical staff fire training and fire warden support.

## LinkedIn

You can have every extinguisher serviced and every alarm tested and still come unstuck in a real incident if people freeze or hesitate. The kit is only half of it. A team that knows what to do is the rest.

Good fire training is not complicated. It covers:

- How to raise the alarm, and what the alarm actually means in your building.
- The evacuation routine: where to go, who checks what, where to gather.
- When it is, and is not, safe to use an extinguisher, and which one.
- The role of a fire warden, and who does it on each shift.

A short, practical session once a year keeps it fresh, and new starters should not wait twelve months for theirs. Delivering training that people actually remember, and that suits your premises, is what we do. Happy to set it up.
