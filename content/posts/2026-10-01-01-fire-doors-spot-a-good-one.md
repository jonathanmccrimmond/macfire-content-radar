## LinkedIn

A fire door is not just a door with a certificate. It is a rated system: the door, the frame, the seals, the closer, and the ironmongery all working together. If any part is wrong, the rating is gone.

Three things worth checking on any fire door you pass:

- The gap around the top and sides should be no more than three millimetres. A coin fit through the gap is a bad sign.
- The intumescent strip (the soft strip in the door or frame) should be continuous, uncut, and not flattened or missing.
- The self-closer should bring the door fully shut from any angle, without being propped, wedged, or held back.

A fire door that fails to close or that has damaged seals can be worse than no plan at all: it gives building users confidence they should not have. Getting the right eyes on them, a proper inspection and certification, is the only way to know they are doing their job. That is something we can sort.

## Facebook

A fire door is only useful if it works. Three quick checks:

- Gap around the edge: no more than 3mm.
- Intumescent seal: continuous, not damaged or missing.
- Self-closer: brings the door fully shut every time.

If any of those fail, the door's rating is gone. Getting them properly inspected and certified is the only way to be sure. We can do that.
