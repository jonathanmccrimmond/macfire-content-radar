## Angle

A monthly two-minute self-check is good practice, but the annual professional service is what keeps a business compliant and covered, and that is what MacFire provides.

## LinkedIn

A quick monthly look at your fire extinguishers takes two minutes and catches the obvious problems. It is worth doing, but it does not replace the annual service, and the service is the part that actually keeps you compliant.

What to glance at each month:

- Pressure gauge needle in the green.
- Each extinguisher in place, clearly visible and unobstructed.
- No obvious damage, corrosion, or a missing pin or tamper seal.
- The service label still in date.

What a monthly look cannot tell you is whether the extinguisher will actually work when it matters. That takes a proper service by a qualified engineer once a year, and it is the bit we look after. If your service is due, or a check turns something up, we are happy to help.
