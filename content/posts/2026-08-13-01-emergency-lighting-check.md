## LinkedIn

Emergency lighting only earns its keep on the day the power goes off, which is exactly why it gets forgotten, and why it needs testing.

The routine is simpler than people expect:

- A short monthly test: briefly cut the power to the units and check each one lights up.
- A full annual test: run them for their rated duration (usually three hours) to confirm the batteries hold.
- Keep a record of each test.

The quick monthly check you can do yourself. The full annual test, and repairing or replacing any unit that fails or fades early, is the professional job, and the bit we handle, along with the certificate that shows it was done. If yours is due, or a unit has stopped working, we can sort it.
