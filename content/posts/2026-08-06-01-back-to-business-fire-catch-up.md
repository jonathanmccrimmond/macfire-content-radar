## Angle

A practical prompt for businesses and facilities teams to catch up on anything that slipped over the summer before autumn gets busy.

## LinkedIn

The post-summer restart is a good moment to catch anything that slipped while people were away.

Worth picking back up:

- Any servicing or maintenance due over the summer: alarms, extinguishers, emergency lighting.
- Alarm and test logs up to date, with any faults from the quiet weeks chased down.
- Fire warden and first-aider cover back to full after holidays and staff changes.
- Summer contractor work signed off, with nothing left blocking routes or wedging doors.

A short catch-up now saves a scramble when autumn gets busy.
