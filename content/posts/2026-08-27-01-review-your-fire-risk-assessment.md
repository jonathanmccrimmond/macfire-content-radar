## LinkedIn

If you run a business or manage a building, you almost certainly need a fire risk assessment, and the part that gets missed is that it is not a one-off.

It is worth a fresh look:

- At least once a year, as a matter of routine.
- After any real change: a new layout, a refit, different use of a space, more people, new equipment or storage.
- After any near miss or incident.

Knowing when to review it is the easy part. The assessment itself needs to be carried out by someone competent, and acted on, because the findings are a to-do list, not a certificate to file away. Carrying out clear, practical fire risk assessments, and helping you close out what they find, is what we do. If yours is overdue, or the building has changed, we can help.
