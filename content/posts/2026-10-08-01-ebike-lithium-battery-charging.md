## Angle

A calm, practical reminder on lithium battery fire risks for the public and employers with delivery or service staff, as darker evenings mean more indoor charging.

## LinkedIn

E-bikes, e-scooters, and the lithium batteries inside them are a real fire risk when they are charged or stored badly. Fires from these batteries spread fast and produce toxic smoke. A few habits make a significant difference.

- Charge during the day when you can keep an eye on it. Unplug when it is done or when you go to sleep.
- Never charge in a hallway, stairwell, or anywhere that blocks an escape route if a fire starts.
- Store and charge at room temperature. Damp, cold, and extreme heat all shorten battery life and raise the risk.
- Buy replacement chargers from the original manufacturer or a verified supplier. Cheap aftermarket chargers are a common cause of fires.
- If a battery gets hot, swells, or smells unusual, stop using it and get advice before charging again.

This applies to staff at premises with delivery riders, hire bikes, or on-site transport too. A brief word with your team is worth it.

## Facebook

As the evenings get darker and bikes come inside more, it is worth a quick reminder on lithium battery safety:

- Charge during the day, unplug before you sleep.
- Never charge in a hallway or anywhere blocking an escape route.
- Store at room temperature, away from damp and heat.
- Only use the original charger or a verified replacement.

A small habit now matters a lot if something goes wrong at 2am.
