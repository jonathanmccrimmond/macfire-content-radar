## Angle

A short reminder for landlords and agents on what fire doors do, why they should never be wedged, and what good condition looks like.

## LinkedIn

Fire doors do a simple job: they hold back smoke and flames long enough for people to get out. They only work when they are closed and in good order.

A quick refresher for landlords, agents, and anyone managing flats or HMOs:

- Self-closers should pull the door fully shut, not leave it resting on the latch.
- Never wedge or prop a fire door open.
- Check the gaps around the door are even and not too wide.
- Look for intumescent strips and cold smoke seals, intact and undamaged.
- Report or replace any door that is split, warped, or missing parts.

If you are not sure a door is doing its job, a competent inspection is worth booking before it becomes a problem.

## Facebook

Fire doors only work when they are closed and in good condition. If you manage flats or an HMO, three quick checks:

- The self-closer pulls the door fully shut.
- Nothing is wedging it open.
- Seals and strips are intact, and the door is not damaged.

Not sure? A competent inspection is worth booking.
