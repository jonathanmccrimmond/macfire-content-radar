## Angle

A general good-practice reminder that keeping escape routes and fire exits clear is one of the easiest and most important fire-safety wins.

## LinkedIn

One of the most effective fire-safety improvements costs nothing at all: keep your escape routes clear.

It is easy for corridors and stairwells to fill up over time. Deliveries, spare furniture, stock, the box that was only meant to be there for an hour. In an evacuation, those are the things people trip over in poor visibility.

A quick walk round, today:

- Nothing stored in corridors, stairwells, or in front of fire exits.
- Every final exit door opens easily from the inside, without a key.
- Fire exit signs are visible, and not hidden behind anything.

If you can walk every escape route from desk to outside without moving a thing, you are in good shape.
