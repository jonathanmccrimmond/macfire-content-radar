## Angle

Poorly positioned smoking areas and unmanaged cigarette waste are a genuine and preventable fire risk. MacFire advises on compliant, practical setups.

## LinkedIn

Cigarette waste is one of the more preventable causes of external building fires, and yet the setup of smoking areas at many commercial premises does not get much thought beyond putting a bin near the door.

A few things that go wrong in practice:

- A metal stub-out bin placed directly against a timber cladding panel, or next to a gas meter.
- A plastic bin used instead of a proper metal-hooded stub-out bin, because it was what was available.
- A smoking shelter that has become an informal storage area for cardboard and packaging.
- Bins that are emptied infrequently, so waste builds up before it is fully out.

The standard is not complicated: a suitable metal bin, at a safe distance from combustible materials and building openings, cleared regularly, in a position that does not encourage smoking near fire exits or deliveries. If your current setup has drifted from that, it is a quick thing to put right. Getting it properly reviewed is something we can do as part of a wider premises assessment.
