## Angle

Fire safety signage is easy to get wrong, and MacFire supplies and fits the right signs, in the right places, to the right standard.

## LinkedIn

Fire safety signage is one of those things that is easy to overlook, until an inspection, or worse, an evacuation, when it suddenly matters a great deal.

A quick look around your premises:

- Can you see a fire exit sign from anywhere a person might be, pointing the right way?
- Are exit and directional signs consistent, not a mix of old and new styles?
- Are the signs that matter in a power cut the photoluminescent or illuminated type?
- Is fire equipment, like extinguishers and call points, clearly identified?
- Nothing faded, hidden behind a noticeboard, or pointing at a door that is now locked?

Spotting the gaps is straightforward. Getting the right signs, in the right places, to the right standard, is the part we handle. If your signage is a patchwork, or the layout has changed, we can put it right.
