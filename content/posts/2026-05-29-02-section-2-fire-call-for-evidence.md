## Angle

Fire guidance is not static. This is a good post for explaining why businesses should keep reviews current.

## LinkedIn

Fire guidance in Scotland is still evolving.

On 16 January 2026, the Scottish Government launched a call for evidence to review Section 2 (Fire) of the Technical Handbooks, following the Grenfell Tower Inquiry response. That matters because fire guidance is not something you file once and forget. It changes as evidence, building practice and lessons from real incidents develop.

For businesses, the practical takeaway is simple: if the building changes, the fire plan should be checked too.
