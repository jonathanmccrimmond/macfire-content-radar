A calm, general reminder of what to do the moment a fire alarm sounds, useful for any workplace or public building.

## LinkedIn

A quick, calm reminder, the kind worth repeating now and then: what to actually do when the fire alarm sounds.

- Treat every alarm as real. Leave straight away.
- Do not stop to collect belongings or finish what you were doing.
- Use the nearest safe exit. Never the lift.
- Help anyone who needs it, and tell a fire warden if you think someone may still be inside.
- Go to the assembly point, and stay there until you are told it is clear.

Most evacuations that go wrong, go wrong in the first thirty seconds, when people hesitate or head the wrong way out of habit. A calm, automatic response is the whole point of practising it.
