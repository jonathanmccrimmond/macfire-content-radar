# Seasonal Content Calendar

Practical 12-month plan for Content Radar posts that are not tied to a new regulation.

The aim is to keep MacFire visible between official updates without sounding noisy or
gimmicky. Posts should be useful reminders that a fire-safety company would naturally
share, mixed across audiences: businesses, landlords, venues, restaurants, care homes,
facilities teams, and the general public where it makes sense.

## Content mix

Each month should aim for roughly:

- **One official update post** when there is genuinely current regulatory, standards,
  or funding news. Skip the month rather than reach for stale material.
- **One seasonal reminder** tied to a predictable risk moment (BBQs, fireworks,
  Christmas shutdown, busy hospitality periods, etc.).
- **One commercial prompt** aimed at businesses, landlords, or facilities teams.
  This can be the same post as the seasonal reminder when the seasonal angle is
  already commercial.
- **One light engagement post** when it fits naturally: a single check, a myth-vs-fact
  reminder, or a short checklist. Optional, not forced.

Target cadence is **one to two posts per week**. News and seasonal posts cover
some of that; the rest comes from the evergreen topic bank below, which is full
of "anytime" fire-safety reminders that can slot into any quiet week. Quality
still comes first: it is better to repeat a strong evergreen reminder than to
publish filler.

## Audience tags

When drafting a post, pick the primary audience so the tone is right:

- **Public** - general safety reminders (BBQs, fireworks, candles).
- **Business** - offices, retail, manufacturing, workshops.
- **Hospitality** - restaurants, cafes, pubs, hotels, venues.
- **Landlord / HMO** - private landlords, letting agents, student lets.
- **Care / Healthcare** - care homes, supported living, GP surgeries.
- **Facilities / Estates** - facilities managers, maintenance, contractors.

Mixing audiences across the month keeps the page from feeling one-note.

4. Approve as normal in the review calendar.

## Engagement formats

Use sparingly so the page does not feel formulaic:

- "Before you lock up on Friday, check this..."
- "One fire-safety job for this month..."
- "Would your team know what to do if..."
- "Myth vs fact: fire doors, extinguishers, alarm servicing, emergency lighting."
- "Three checks before a busy weekend in hospitality."
- "Seasonal checklist" style posts for Facebook and LinkedIn.

## Guardrails

- Keep posts short and specific.
- Avoid fear-based or gimmicky copy.
- Do not present seasonal tips as legal advice. Where the law is relevant,
  point to the responsible person and suggest a competent review.
- For any claim that depends on a current regulation, restriction, or scheme,
  add a public source with its publication date.
- Evergreen posts can be reused annually, but always run them past the reviewer
  before scheduling.
- Skip a month rather than publish filler.
